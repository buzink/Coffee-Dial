Privacy Policy

Coffee Dial · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Coffee Dial is a web app for logging home coffee brews, bags, coffees and gear, with optional photos, statistics and friends who can follow each other's brews. It is made by two home brewers, Tieme and Robert. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have.

1. Who is responsible

Coffee Dial is operated by Tieme and Robert. You can contact us using the details in section 11.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google, and a Firebase user ID that identifies your account in the app. We rely on the email address being verified by Google.
  • Coffee data you enter. The coffees, bags, brews, gear, recipes, ratings, tasting notes and brew measurements (including scale readings from a brew graph) that you save.
  • Photos. Photos you upload, such as pictures of coffee packaging, brews or your setup. Photos are stored in your own storage area and are only visible to you unless you share them with friends.
  • Social settings. If you enable a public profile, your display name and a share ID are visible to other users. Friend requests, follows and blocks are stored so the app can work.
  • Notification tokens. If you allow notifications, a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging is stored so that we can send you push notifications.
  • Gemini API key (optional). If you use the AI features, you can enter your own Google Gemini API key in Preferences. The key is stored in your account (so it syncs between your devices) and in the browser's local storage on the device you use.
  • Usage analytics. The website uses Google Analytics 4 to count page views and general usage (for example which pages are visited, and from which country or device type). This is aggregated and is used only to improve the app.
  • Technical data. Standard server and browser data, such as IP address and request times, that Google Cloud and Firebase process when the app is used.

3. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your data separate from other users' data.
  • To store, show, edit and back up your coffee data across your devices.
  • To show your statistics, analyses and Coffee Card views.
  • To run the social features you choose to use, such as following friends and sharing photos.
  • To send push notifications you have agreed to receive.
  • To run the AI features you choose to use (see section 5).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To understand general usage of the website and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Google user data and the Limited Use requirements

When you sign in with Google, Coffee Dial requests access to your basic profile information (your name, email address and profile photo) through the openid, email and profile scopes. We use this data only to sign you in, identify your account and show your name and profile photo in the app. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or other Google data.

Coffee Dial's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the service, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of the service with the same protections. We do not use Google user data to serve advertising, and humans do not read it unless you give us permission, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.

5. AI features and third parties

Some features (scanning a bag from a photo, reading a coffee's web page, and an AI taste profile) send the content you choose to Google's Gemini API, directly from your browser, using the Gemini API key you entered. Google processes that content under its own terms and privacy policy. Only use these features with content you are comfortable sending to Google. You can remove your key at any time in Preferences.

When you use "Extract from URL", the address you enter may be fetched by a Coffee Dial Cloud Function on Google Cloud, which downloads the product photo from that web page. The function only fetches public web pages.

We use the following service providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging) for accounts, data, photos and notifications.
  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run and Cloud Functions) for backend services.
  • Google Analytics 4 for aggregated usage statistics.
  • Google Gemini API, only when you use the AI features with your own key.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website files.

We do not share your personal information with anyone else for their own purposes.

6. Sharing with other users

Coffee Dial is social by design, but you control it. Your data is private by default. Other users see your brews, photos or profile only when you choose to share them, for example by enabling a public profile, sharing a photo with friends, or accepting a friend request. You can block a user, remove a follower, or stop sharing at any time.

7. Where data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Cloud and Firebase data centres. Google may store data in regions outside your own country, under Google's data protection safeguards.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete an item in the app, it is removed from your data. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and the data stored under it, except for records we must keep by law.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. You can see and change your data in the app at any time.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to delete your account and your data (see section 11).
  • Revoke Google access. You can remove Coffee Dial's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your stored data stays until you ask us to delete it.
  • Notifications and analytics. You can turn off push notifications in your browser or in the app. You can block Google Analytics with a browser extension or your browser's privacy settings.
  • Other rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area), you may have further rights, such as data portability, restriction or objection. Contact us to use them, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Coffee Dial is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
